Collision: when two or more signals meet on the medium they become scrambled and the data is damaged.
Use the "Send Data" button to send a packet from the User node and watch collisions with the Bot node (the Bot sends on its own at random intervals).
Notes about this page's behavior:
- K value counts collisions and now accumulates across transmissions (it is not reset when a packet reaches the bot).
- The K-check input and "Check" button are disabled until you send at least one packet (they become enabled after the first Send Data).
- User-sent signals are shown in red; bot signals are cyan by default.
- The bot sends its first signal quickly (within the first second) and then continues sending at randomized longer intervals.
